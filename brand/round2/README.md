### Search method and dropped names

For every candidate, searched the exact name with AI, creative tools, image, video, app, software and generator terms. A second pass used quoted “image generator,” “video generator” and “video editor” combinations for ambiguous names. Reviewed product-owner pages where a match appeared. The searches distinguish products from surnames, papers, stock-image tags and agencies.

Dropped before the final twelve:

- **Eikonal:** an existing [visual art instrument](https://afolkestad.com/projects/eikonal.html).
- **Bruma:** a direct [AI video model/product name](https://www.picassoia.com/en/collection/text-to-video/picassoia-bruma-reference-to-video).

## Concept 1: Fulvo

**Direction:** abstract geometric symbol.  
**Board:** [concept-1-fulvo.png](concept-1-fulvo.png).  
**Selection:** exploration E, after five generated variations.

Two staggered semicircles create a compact sign through a straight diagonal gap and displaced centres. Circular outer contours meet crisp internal edges, giving it tension without a camera, graph or workflow metaphor. The name supplies warmth, while the mark stays formally simple. The black Explore application and quarry image test whether the identity can sit beside cinematic work without competing with it.

**Rejected:** A resembles a coffee bean. B suggests an ear. C looks like paired beans or leaves. D becomes an organic bone. E has the clearest construction and silhouette, though split-circle marks are common in technology. The offset must survive a precise vector redraw; the raster applications vary slightly.

## Concept 2: Velri

**Direction:** custom wordmark only.  
**Board:** [concept-2-velri.png](concept-2-velri.png).  
**Selection:** exploration B, from three generated variations.

The identity resides in five letters: an angled e crossbar, wedge terminals, a tall l and square i dot. Orange serif lettering gives the tool an editorial character and makes the cover feel calm without an extra symbol. Fabric and directional stage light extend that character into a cinematic setting. The same full word appears on the icon tiles, preserving the direction honestly.

**Rejected:** A is readable but too close to a stock humanist sans. C overstates its angled terminals and turns the r into an arrow-like gesture. B has the strongest rhythm. Its limitation is real: the full word loses legibility at 16 px, and the thin details soften at 32 px. This is the strongest identity direction, but the weakest favicon proposition. The existing health brand also limits name ownership.

## Concept 3: Ewald

**Direction:** letter monogram reshaped into an icon.  
**Board:** [concept-3-ewald.png](concept-3-ewald.png).  
**Selection:** exploration A, from three generated variations.

A convex spine and three tapered arms reshape the E into a compact, open silhouette. The central arm is shorter, leaving clear counterspaces and a forward rhythm. A restrained neutral wordmark lets the letter carry the character. Motion photography tests it in a plausible creator environment, while the flat icon tests keep the geometry independent of lighting effects.

**Rejected:** B becomes a sports or mobility emblem through its diagonal terminals and thick spine. C is a conventional serif E with decorative scoops. A is more fluid and better balanced, but it can read as an epsilon and still has an automotive association. The middle arm needs optical attention at tiny sizes. Fourth place reflects those limits.

## Concept 4: Zernike

**Direction:** reduced optical motif.  
**Board:** [concept-4-zernike.png](concept-4-zernike.png).  
**Selection:** exploration B, from three generated variations.

A circular pupil interrupted by two offset horizontal slots suggests phase displacement through a single flat sign. It is inspired by optical contrast, not a literal diagram of Zernike's microscope. The opposing slot entries give the circle character without a ring, orbit or aperture-blade assembly. Orange light scattering belongs to the photographic application; the logo remains solid ink.

**Rejected:** A reads as a G or power-control symbol. C is an eccentric ring whose thin rim disappears too quickly at small size. B has the strongest compact silhouette and the simplest reversal. Its risks are the crowded scientific name and a slightly sleepy-face reading of the slots. A final vector version should tune the slot widths at 16 px.

## Honest ranking

1. **Velri:** strongest visual personality and best departure from round 1. The wordmark does the work. Existing health-brand use and favicon legibility are material drawbacks.
2. **Zernike:** strongest compact symbol and clearest optical foundation. Harder name to say and spell; established adjacent optics use.
3. **Fulvo:** warm, concise name and cleanest geometric construction after substantial rejection. Split-circle familiarity and the Volvo sound association keep it below the first two.
4. **Ewald:** usable monogram, but the most familiar lettermark and the most industrial overall. Its automotive imagery reinforces that reading.

**Recommendation:** develop Velri's lettering direction further if the priority is a distinct creative identity. Choose Zernike's symbol direction if the compact app mark matters most. Neither is presented as a cleared launch name.
