# Round 3: Velri, Phaino, Kinora

**My pick: Velri A.** It retains the serif character selected in round 2 and gives the app a compact V instead of shrinking the full name. The six boards follow nineteen separate image-tool explorations. Names remain in the requested preference order.

## Velri A

[velri-a.png](velri-a.png). Selected exploration: [3](iterations/velri-a-3.png). A single serif V uses the wordmark's wedge shoulders, weighted left diagonal and open counter. The angled e crossbar, tall l and square i dot retain the editorial character of round 2; the final spelling is title-case Velri as requested. Orange silk and stage light connect the identity to image making. Rejected [1](iterations/velri-a-1.png) because its detached right arm introduces an unnecessary break, and [2](iterations/velri-a-2.png) because the Ve ligature crowds the icon. The final V is coherent and readable, but a serif V alone is familiar. The wordmark supplies most of the personality, and its finer terminals soften at small sizes.

## Velri B

[velri-b.png](velri-b.png). Selected exploration: [2](iterations/velri-b-2.png). Two upright image planes sit at different heights with a curved internal seam, paired with a sturdy custom semi-serif wordmark. Their broad silhouettes give the icon more body than Velri A; suspended projection panels carry the same idea into the imagery. Rejected [1](iterations/velri-b-1.png) because its rounded diagonal-cut square feels like a general app icon, and [3](iterations/velri-b-3.png) because the stepped channel reads as code brackets. This is the bolder alternative, although the paired planes can also read as quotation marks. Their narrow meeting point needs optical adjustment in a production master.

## Phaino A

[phaino-a.png](phaino-a.png). Selected exploration: [1](iterations/phaino-a-1.png). A broad diagonal opening reveals two parts of an image plane: a curved upper surface and a grounded lower block. Light makes orange pigment visible against darkness in the cinematic application. The mark stays flat and separate from that photographic effect. Rejected [2](iterations/phaino-a-2.png) because it reads as a D or a door, and [3](iterations/phaino-a-3.png) because its tapered opening resembles a road. The selected silhouette reduces clearly, but it can read as a Z and belongs to a familiar family of divided-plane symbols. Its meaning is stronger than its distinctiveness.

## Phaino B

[phaino-b.png](phaino-b.png). Selected exploration: [3](iterations/phaino-b-3.png). An upright stem and detached upper bowl form a P with an open horizontal counter. The opening expresses revealing an image without adding another object to the letter. The rounded inner shoulder and softly cut wordmark keep the result calm; a portrait emerging into orange sidelight supplies the brand moment. Rejected [1](iterations/phaino-b-1.png) because its boxy slit feels like a control symbol and nearly closes at the right edge, and [2](iterations/phaino-b-2.png) because the diagonal cut pushes it toward an energy emblem. This is the clearest compact monogram after Velri A. The risk is familiarity with rounded software lettermarks, and the stem-to-bowl gap needs careful optical sizing.

## Kinora A

[kinora-a.png](kinora-a.png). Selected exploration: [4](iterations/kinora-a-4.png), generated after the first three did not clear the bar. A broad curved hook and smaller following plane suggest a picture moving to its next rotational position. A dancer's turn makes that sequence tangible without a reel illustration. Rejected [1](iterations/kinora-a-1.png) because it is too close to Velri B's paired planes, [2](iterations/kinora-a-2.png) because its opposing arcs resemble a sync control, and [3](iterations/kinora-a-3.png) because it reads as two speed bars. The fourth pass has a stronger compact silhouette, but an F, partial G or interface-control reading remains possible. The dance imagery explains motion more strongly than the mark does, which keeps this direction last.

## Kinora B

[kinora-b.png](kinora-b.png). Selected exploration: [2](iterations/kinora-b-2.png). A sturdy serif K has a gently turning upper arm and a calmer lower diagonal, leaving two open viewing angles around the junction. The monogram connects to the editorial wordmark and stays upright. Orange fabric in motion keeps the application in a creative studio rather than motorsport. Rejected [1](iterations/kinora-b-1.png) because the detached stem reads as I plus a chevron, and [3](iterations/kinora-b-3.png) because its blockier arm assembly looks more like a sports emblem. The selected K is warmer, but its flared upper arm can suggest a leaf or wing. It is readable without being the most distinctive identity here.

## Honest ranking

1. **Velri A:** best continuation of the direction already selected. The compact V solves the previous full-word icon problem, while the lettering retains the character.
2. **Phaino B:** clearest alternative monogram and most direct use of negative space. Slightly familiar software-brand territory.
3. **Velri B:** stronger abstract app silhouette and bolder lettering. Less connected to the prior serif preference, with a possible quotation-mark reading.
4. **Kinora B:** readable, typographically coherent and more human than the other K explorations. The flared arm needs restraint.
5. **Phaino A:** clear reveal idea and simple reduction. A Z reading and divided-plane familiarity weaken ownership.
6. **Kinora A:** improved after regeneration, but its meaning still depends too much on the photographic application.
