# Version 3: Veil and Refraction

Made with the built-in Image Gen tool, using brandkit direction for the existing Phaino identity. Original approved v1 layouts were supplied as edit targets. Generated PNGs are retained in this directory, and the build script exports them at 1200 × 630 for social embeds.

## Veil

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a new art-directed version of the supplied Phaino social preview. This is an existing identity extension using brandkit design discipline. The reference is authoritative for the logo, wordmark, wording and centered layout, but its background is only a rough placeholder.
Deliver ONE finished horizontal social card, target 1200 by 630 pixels, aspect ratio 1.90476:1. No board, mockup or surrounding border.
Art direction: a contemporary independent design studio's quiet identity cover for a creative image-making tool. Beautiful photographic abstraction, considered composition and rich negative space. Matte, atmospheric, tactile, editorial. A softly diffused analog orange light exposure occupies only the bottom third of the black canvas. Build it from two or three large overlapping translucent fields with beautifully controlled gradients and a very subtle diagonal taper, as though warm orange light is being veiled behind several sheets of frosted film. Broad soft shapes and nuanced tonal transitions, one darker translucent layer passing across the light. The most saturated orange is near the bottom right while the left fades softly to deep burnt orange. The transition to black is long and elegant. Extremely fine analog grain ties it together. The artwork should be sophisticated enough to be the cover of a contemporary graphic design monograph.
Preserve the original flat orange Phaino logo with its exact two curved shapes and open diagonal band, plus the correct geometric white Phaino wordmark. Preserve this arrangement: centered horizontal mark and wordmark near the middle, secondary descriptor underneath, tiny footer URL at bottom center. Refine the overall scale down about 10 percent for more breathing room, keeping all text beautifully sharp.
Text exactly and only: "Phaino", "AI video and image studio", "phaino.us.ci".
Palette: true black #000000, orange #FF5B22 plus restrained darker orange tones, clean white type and warm gray descriptor. Upper 60 percent should remain essentially pure black. Keep all type on calm dark areas. Flat logo, no glow or texture on logo or type.
Strictly exclude: luminous sharp horizon lines, planetary sunrise, lasers, neon, glass sculptures, shiny metal, glossy 3D render, flowing ribbons, smoke clouds, flames, liquid, toruses, spheres, waves, busy textures, sparkles, extra symbols, extra slogans, outlines, interface details. Do NOT render a landscape, space scene or obvious physical object. It is a restrained photographic light study and an excellent typographic composition.

## Refraction

Use case: ads-marketing. Redesign the BACKGROUND of this approved Phaino social card with art direction at the level of a contemporary independent graphic design studio. Extend the existing identity using brandkit principles. Preserve the reference's left-aligned structure and exact existing Phaino symbol and wordmark; do not design a new logo.
Output ONE standalone horizontal social image, 1200 x 630 target, ratio 1.90476:1. No multi-panel board, border or device mockup.
Concept: "diffused image plane". Artful abstract photography of warm orange light refracted through closely spaced, extremely fine vertical frosted-glass ridges. The abstraction fills the right 43 percent from top to bottom, cropped at the right edge. One broad soft orange exposure with an oblique shadow passing across it, split into hundreds of fine softly defined parallel vertical optical lines. Vary the light height across the ridges to make a quiet asymmetric fan or blurred diagonal falloff, with a dark upper-right area and rich saturated light at lower right. This is a flat photographic surface, subtly translucent, tactile and sophisticated. Broad blur is primary, fine ribbing is a secondary detail. Use gentle analog grain, beautiful restrained black-to-burnt-orange-to-Phaino-orange tonal transitions. High-end editorial identity artwork for a visual creation tool. Keep the left 56 percent true black with a very soft seamless transition into the artwork, preserving calm negative space for text. No hard panel divider.
Composition: small flat orange logo at upper left, large crisp white "Phaino" at middle left, warm-gray descriptor beneath it, discreet URL at bottom left. Refine type scale slightly smaller than the reference for space and balance. Preserve the supplied flat symbol: two orange curved filled shapes separated by the original open diagonal band. No symbol reinterpretation. Text exactly: "Phaino"; "AI video and image studio"; "phaino.us.ci". Original geometric Outfit-style wordmark in white, accurate single-story a.
Palette strictly true black #000000, Phaino orange #FF5B22 and dark ember tints, white and gray type. Warm orange light with no yellow-white or pink hot spots.
Avoid: recognisable 3D objects, shiny metal, rubber blobs, flowing silk ribbons, laser beams, horizon rims, flames, smoke, planets, stars, neon, rings, spheres, topographic lines, busy patterns, software dashboards, extra text or slogans, glows on logo or type. The feel is a very considered Swiss editorial poster with beautiful experimental photographic material.
